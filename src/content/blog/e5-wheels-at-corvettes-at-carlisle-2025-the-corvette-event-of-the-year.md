Get ready, Corvette fans -- Corvettes at Carlisle 2025 is set to ignite the summer with horsepower, heritage, and high-octane excitement! From August 21-23, 2025, this world-renowned event returns to the Carlisle Fairgrounds in Carlisle, Pennsylvania, bringing together over 3,000 Corvettes and thousands of passionate enthusiasts from across the country.

E5 Wheels is proud to announce that we'll be there, showcasing our latest premium wheels and designs for the C6, C7, and C8 Corvette. Stop by our booth to see how we're redefining performance and style for America's sports car.

## Event Details

- **Dates:** August 21-23, 2025
- **Location:** Carlisle Fairgrounds, 1000 Bryn Mawr Rd, Carlisle, PA 17013
- **Gates Open:** 7:00 AM daily

## Tickets & Admission

Spectator tickets start at $20/day or grab a 3-day event pass for just $35. Kids 12 and under get in free. Save time and money by purchasing your tickets in advance through the Carlisle Events website. Special discounts are available for military personnel and first responders.

## Where to Stay

Carlisle and the surrounding Cumberland Valley area offer a wide range of lodging, from national hotel chains to charming inns and campgrounds. Book early -- this event draws huge crowds. The closest hotels are located along Route 11 and Interstate 81, all just minutes from the fairgrounds.

## What to Expect at Corvettes at Carlisle 2025

This is more than a car show -- it's a full-throttle celebration of the Corvette legacy. Here's a glimpse of what you'll experience:

- **The Corvette Fun Field:** Join thousands of Corvette owners on the Showfield. Display your ride or admire everything from vintage Sting Rays to the latest C8 Z06s.
- **Chip's Choice Display:** A specially curated indoor exhibit celebrating unique or historically significant Corvettes.
- **Exhaust Contest:** Hear the roar! Watch as Corvette owners compete for the loudest and most aggressive exhaust tones.
- **Autocross Track:** Test your driving skills or watch others take on the course in timed competition.
- **Installations & Aftermarket Alley:** Browse wheels, parts, detailing services, and more. Don't forget -- E5 Wheels will be there offering the perfect custom upgrade for your Corvette!
- **Corvette Parade & Street Party:** On Saturday night, over 400 Corvettes cruise into downtown Carlisle for a high-energy street party.
- **Raffles & Giveaways:** Including the chance to win a brand-new Corvette!
